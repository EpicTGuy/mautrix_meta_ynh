* A Synapse instance must be installed on the server.
* The **bridge administrator** must be a single Matrix account (e.g. `@john:example.com`). Do not put the same account in the **authorized users** field: use your server name there (e.g. `example.com`) to authorize all local accounts.
* Login is done from the bot chat, with your username and password (Messenger app API) or with your facebook.com / messenger.com browser cookies. Meta may flag or temporarily lock accounts using unofficial clients: use at your own risk.
* Upstream documentation: <https://docs.mau.fi/bridges/go/meta/index.html> — support room: `#meta:maunium.net`
